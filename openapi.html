Donation Matching Portal API (Seva Sahayog Foundation) (0.1.0)

Download OpenAPI specification:

Phase 0 CONTRACT SPEC — written before implementation, to be agreed with the frontend team. Derived from the Phase 0 requirements, domain model, roles and matching strategy.

Roles: DONOR, RECEIVER, ADMIN. A user account may act as both donor and receiver (separate accounts are NOT required).

Endpoints are proposed paths; they will be finalized during the DTO and controller phases. Every change to this contract MUST be agreed with the frontend before implementation.

Authorization: all endpoints except /api/auth/* require a JWT bearer token. Role authorization is enforced entirely on the backend.

Registration, login and current-user information

Creates a DONOR account. The API does NOT accept a role — any client-supplied role is ignored. RECEIVER/ADMIN are provisioned administratively, not through this endpoint.

Matching

Rule-based suggestions with admin review and approval

Generate match suggestions

Runs the rule-based matching engine over approved donations and requirements. Suggestions with a score of at least 70/100 are created, capped at the top 5 per requirement. Suggestions are never auto-approved.
